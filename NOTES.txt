Mech Royale bot by Claude ("oval-ghost") -- LATEST: version 4
==============================================================
This folder always holds the newest version that beat the previous one. Each version passed
the OFFICIAL kit's check.py (joinbuilders.org mechfight-kit.tar.gz) natively AND in the Docker
sandbox (--network none --cpus 1 --memory 512m --read-only --tmpfs /tmp --user 65534:65534).

HOW TO SUBMIT
- Put every file of this folder at the TOP LEVEL of the public GitHub repo you submit
  (Dockerfile at the top), commit, push, submit the repo URL on the Mech Royale track page.
- mech-royale-claude.zip = the same files with the folder layout (sim/ inside).
- Runtime files: Dockerfile, bot.py, arena.py, mechfight.py. Keep LICENSE (MIT) and README.md.
  sim/ is a local test tool, not part of the Docker image.
- Older files are kept in the "Old versions" subfolder, renamed with their version number.
- Git: these are commits on local branch claude-bot of jpptauriarg/mech-royale-bot
  (not pushed: this session has no GitHub write access).

SCORES_AND_TIPS.md / TACTICS.md (from grokbot) read before this version: real practice score
of the old main is 2.40 (rank 311/360); local sims are much harsher than the real house bots;
house bots act as one squad (shared vision, 1 hunter early, more as you kill, all late).

                   Docker sandbox PASS (300/300 in time, p99 13.8 ms, max 15.3 ms).
What changed vs version 3:
- Fights whenever it is loaded and the target is in range, even if a second mech can see it
  (it still picks the best spot and shoots whoever is hitting it first). Hiding from hunters
  that come to us anyway only hands them free shots and time to regenerate.
- Upgrades: plating (+15% max HP) and nanites (faster regen) before armor. Armor is only
  ~8.7% effective HP and does nothing against the zone, which decides the final circle.
  (Not measurable in the simulator, which has no upgrades.)

VERSION 3  (commit 205500c; simulator squad mode in the next commit)
Official check.py: native PASS (300/300 in time, p99 11.8 ms, max 14.2 ms);
                   Docker sandbox PASS (300/300 in time, p99 14.7 ms, max 15.3 ms).
What changed vs version 2:
- Loot priority laser > lightning. In the simulator, matches where the bot held a laser
  averaged 4.4 points vs 0.4 without one (laser = hitscan 315 per charge, out to 280 m).
- With no laser in the first 3 minutes and no threat near, it walks between points 60-140 m
  away inside the safe circle (torso sweeping) to find one; remembered lasers count to 200 m.
- Laser aim leads the target 0.1 s (it is hitscan, not a bullet); a charge starts only when
  on target or about to peek a target in range. Laser hit rate 62% -> 66%.
- Tested and rejected: aiming along the direction of travel while moving (scored 2.28 vs 2.70
  on 96 seeds: seeing threats late costs more than it saves).

VERSION 2  (commit 2630488)
Official check.py: native PASS (300/300 in time, p99 16.0 ms, max 16.0 ms);
                   Docker sandbox PASS (300/300 in time, p99 17.2 ms, max 17.4 ms).
What changed vs version 1:
- Fights back harder. Measured in the simulator, v1 hit 87% of its gun shots but fired too
  little and often died with its target nearly dead (126-302 HP left). Now it engages whenever
  exactly one enemy can see it and it is loaded (gun out to 160 m), never breaks off from a
  target under 35% HP, and finishes a low target even with a second enemy watching.
- Faces attackers it cannot see (turns to the damage bearing) and targets whoever is hitting
  it. v1 sometimes kept aiming at a harmless enemy while another shot it from outside its view.
- Ranges unseen attackers from the matching gunshot sound (near/mid/far) instead of a guess.
- Line of fire tries centre, upper body and legs, and trusts an open line while being hit.
- Laser alert: when a visible enemy charges a laser at it, it breaks line of sight at once.
- Cornered (one enemy close, no cover within 10 m): fights instead of showing its back.
- Loot: remembers every lightning/laser pickup it has seen, sweeps its torso in the first
  75 s to find them, loots dead enemies' weapons. Lightning first: each hit shorts a laser
  (drops its charge, no new one for 2 s), and the house bots carry lasers.
- Commits to a hiding spot for 0.6 s (v1 could flip-flop between two spots under fire).
- Path planning now stops on a real deadline: worst tick 16-19 ms (v1 up to ~40 ms when the
  test machine was busy).
